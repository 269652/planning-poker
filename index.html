Planning Poker

Estimate Jira tickets together, in realtime — one session per board. Rounds repeat until the team agrees, then the estimate is written back to Jira.

How it works

  • Hosting a room needs the Jira helper, a small program that runs on the host's computer and is connected to Jira with the host's credentials. The room itself runs in the host's browser tab — keep it open during the session.
  • Guests need nothing but the invite link: no Jira access, no setup. Everyone connects to the host through a small relay; tickets and results are streamed from the host.
